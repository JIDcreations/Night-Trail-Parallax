## Original work

- `assets/own-fernsehturm.webp`, `assets/own-streetlamp.webp`,
  `assets/own-tower-dawn.webp`: Berlin photographs by the site's author
  (iPhone, July). Graded like the rest; camera and GPS metadata removed.

- `assets/svg/*.svg`: silhouettes (skylines, catenary masts, platforms, tree
  lines, power poles, industry, station canopy). Generated by
  `tools/make-silhouettes.py`, no third-party artwork.
- Film grain, vignette, flares, window reflection and rain streaks are pure CSS.
- The rain texture's brightness was converted to transparency (no blend mode needed).
